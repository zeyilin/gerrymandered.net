Gerrymandered

About Gerrymandered

Gerrymandered was created by Yash Aggarwal, Sami Glasco, and Zeyi Lin during the weekend of January 15 - 17, 2016, for the 2016 HackRice hackathon.

The website helps users find information about their elected representatives in the U.S. Congress. Enter your address or use your browser's geolocation to discover your local House representative and both of your U.S. Senators, along with their contact information and social media profiles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is gerrymandering?

Gerrymandering is the practice of drawing electoral district boundaries to favor a particular political party or group. The term originated in 1812, when Massachusetts Governor Elbridge Gerry signed a bill creating a contorted voting district that was said to resemble a salamander.

How do I use this site?

Simply enter your U.S. street address on the home page, or click the location icon to use your browser's geolocation. The site will show you your local House representative and both of your state's U.S. Senators, along with their party, office location, website, and social media profiles.

Where does the data come from?

Representative data is sourced from public government APIs that provide information about current members of the U.S. Congress.
